# What the AI Pilot Readiness Assessment demonstrates

**A free, open-source first-pass review of an AI pilot before wider use is considered.**

An AI demonstration can look convincing without answering the operational questions: was the test representative, were mistakes reviewed, does someone own the workflow, is its data use approved, and can it be stopped safely?

The [AI Pilot Readiness Assessment](https://shaunmarsden.github.io/practical-ai-adoption/pilot-readiness/) gives a business manager ten guided questions, highlights missing safeguards, and generates a PDF that records the answers and outstanding checks. It runs in the browser without an AI API, account or upload of answers.

**A fictional example:** a service-request sorting pilot records promising test results, but its data permission is still unknown. The rules return **DO NOT EXPAND** despite the other recorded Yes answers. This is an illustration, not an actual client result or evidence of money saved.

**What it demonstrates:** a workflow-first way of assessing AI adoption, explicit review gates, deterministic decisions, reproducible tests and human accountability. [Read the decision rules and tests](../tools/pilot-readiness/README.md).

**What it cannot establish:** whether a written evidence note is true, whether a pilot is legally compliant, whether the organisation will adopt the workflow, or whether expansion will pay off. Even READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW means a responsible person must examine real evidence before deciding. [Read the methodology critique](pilot-readiness-methodology-review.md).

This project is independently developed. OpenAI, Anthropic and Google did not design or endorse the checker. Their [published guidance](official-ai-adoption-resources.md) is reading material, not validation of this tool.
