A recipient who skims only the first screen of a forwarded C2 record could notice the paired positive-looking choices and miss the missing authority further down. The prominent opening warning, repeated self-reported labels and immediate warning about insufficient authority directly counter that reading. Severity: unestablished; at most a candidate minor presentation risk on current evidence. Confidence: low that it causes actual misunderstanding.
Smallest change if later observed: move or repeat the existing evidence basis and permission/decision authority fields into REVIEW THESE FIRST, retaining 'Not recorded' and the user's exact restrictions.
