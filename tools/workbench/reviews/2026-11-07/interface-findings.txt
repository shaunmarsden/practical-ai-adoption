Overall judgement
No blocking approval-conversion defect was demonstrated. The notebook allows unsupported and contradictory self-reports, as its wording explicitly promises, but does not convert them into a verified readiness result or operational approval. The strongest residual risk is selective reading or selective quotation of positive-sounding user entries. That is a hypothesis about human interpretation, not observed confusion. One small presentation improvement is justified for consideration, not as a proven safety failure.

Low-priority presentation opportunity: positive selections are highlighted ahead of their specific supporting references
Severity: low (P3), optional hardening, not a release blocker established by this review.
Confidence: high in observable behaviour; medium-low in the interpretation risk.

Realistic possible consequence: someone who reads or forwards only the short REVIEW THESE FIRST block could overlook that the evidence is fictional and that the purported decision lacks a recorded authority reference. This is a possible misunderstanding, not one observed in a human test. The block already says self-reported and puts adverse findings first, and the caveat and literal-copy warning substantially reduce this risk.
Smallest justified improvement: add Evidence basis, V2 review reference and Decision-maker role/authority reference to REVIEW THESE FIRST, preserving Not recorded for blanks. A decision date can also help. Do not label such references verified, require a positive decision, calculate readiness or claim to check authority. No new approval gate is justified by this review alone.

Protective wording and behaviour observed
1. Completion is navigation, not certification. Five Next clicks with every field untouched reach Record a human decision; Next becomes disabled. No completion badge or approval message is created. Defaults stay Evidence basis: Not recorded, V2: Not reviewed, Human decision: No human decision recorded. Blank export preserves these defaults and marks other notes Not recorded. Source: content.mjs:45,57,67,77-78; app.mjs:58-67. Evidence: interface/blank-preview.txt, interface/blank-export.txt and observations.json.
2. The manual V2 control identifies itself as self-reported, says to leave Not reviewed unless V2 was used, and explicitly says a complete record is never approval. Its section says the notebook does not run V2 rules, transfer answers or validate the result. Source: content.mjs:53-59. The V2 link has target=_blank and rel=noopener noreferrer. The test inspected this link without opening or executing the separate assessment.
3. A human decision is recorded only as reported. The section says choosing an option grants no authority, resolves no issues and authorises no action. No decision is selected on the user's behalf, and the choices include stop, gather evidence and non-AI routes. Source: content.mjs:63-71. This is stronger protection than merely warning at export time.
4. Optional notes are honest about omissions. The export section explicitly says no fields are required, blanks say Not recorded and export does not complete a review. The blank and contradictory exports retain unanswered items; they do not insert permission, authority or dates. Source: index.html:32-38; content.mjs:75-87. Allowing incomplete export is not by itself an approval defect in this notebook.
5. Adverse findings survive positive selections. In the populated fictional case, the export begins with the explicit no-approval caveat, then the recorded invented-date failures and missing permissions under Known problems, followed by self-reported selections. It also says notes are not checked for truth, consistency or sufficient authority and that this is not a V2 assessment report. Source: content.mjs:75,82-84; interface/contradictory-export.txt.
6. Mobile preserves the important qualifiers. At 320 x 740 CSS pixels the native select crops the V2 choice, but the full selected string directly underneath wraps across three lines, including HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED. The human decision's full authorised-boundary wording also wraps below its control. Both screens have document scrollWidth 320, matching viewport width. Source: app.mjs:43-49; interface/mobile-v2.png, interface/mobile-decision.png and observations.json. This is browser emulation and screenshot inspection, not a physical-device or human comprehension study.
7. Export status says only Text record download requested, with advice to check downloads. It does not say review completed, approved or ready. Source: app.mjs:83-98; observed in the populated run.
