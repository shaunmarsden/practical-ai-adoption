## Static assessment and limits

observeAssets writes final observations into report.assetsAfter. The wrapper checks every final asset before assigning passed. Network or comparison failures reach the catch/finally path, so partial final observations and failed status can be saved. Initial mismatches still stop before journeys. This update closes the demonstrated possibility that the tested deployment changes between the initial scan and completed journeys.

Two matching scans cannot prove the deployment remained unchanged at every moment between them or cannot change immediately afterwards. This is bounded independent AI/software review of release-verification infrastructure, not human validation, a real workplace trial, production verification or a guarantee about future deployment state. Earlier negative-case records remain untouched.
