Privacy and limits

Answers stay in page memory. The app uses no browser storage, analytics, account or AI service and does not upload answers. GitHub Pages receives ordinary page and asset requests; answers are not included. All PDF assets are served with this site. Refreshing or closing clears app memory, though the browser may restore form values through session recovery. Exported JSON and PDF files persist on your device. Handle them under your organisation's rules.

The tool cannot verify notes, detect fabrication or infer risk from prose. False Yes or no-issue claims can still mislead. An independent responsible person must inspect the records and applicable requirements. No result permits a pilot to continue or deploy without its own approvals.
