AI Adoption Workbench

Describe one job, compare ways to improve it and keep the evidence behind a human decision.

Start by describing the work. This is a route through existing guidance, not a new assessment or a fixed method. Skip steps that do not apply and record why. Safety and permission apply throughout.

Use fictional or non-sensitive notes and safe evidence references. Do not enter employer, customer, personal or confidential details. Notes stay in this open page and are lost on reload or closing. Export if you need to keep them. There is no login, telemetry, backend or AI API.

Guide links open GitHub; V2 opens a separate tab. No notes are passed to either. Downloads may be retained or synced by your device. Review them before sharing.
