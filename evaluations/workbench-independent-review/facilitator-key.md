# Facilitator key - do not give to reviewers beforehand

**Status: designed expectations, not observed results.** Keep the original reader answers separate. These examples are illustrative interpretations, not a new automated score or permission framework.

## Scenario expectations

**A.** The reviewer should at least consider a shared template or single date source, fixing ownership or process consistency without AI. AI might be considered, but is not necessarily preferable. Missing evidence includes why dates diverge, how updates are authorised and whether a simpler solution works. An unsupported invented numeric saving is an error.

**B.** Faster drafting alone is insufficient. Checking/rework, rejected cases, comparable full-process time, failure severity and real-data permission remain unknown or adverse. The wrong deadline must not disappear from the record merely because the claimed benefit sounds positive. The review is not completed by entering V2's outcome text in a Workbench field.

**C.** The chosen existing decision label may be 'Gather evidence, repeat or narrow', with clarification in the rationale that invented-data work alone is contemplated. A decision to gather evidence is not permission to use real records. The reviewer should locate 'Export text record'; export success is observed separately from their understanding of the record.

## Comprehension checks

1. **No.** The Workbench is an unverified notebook, not a safety, permission or completion gate.
2. A claim is user-entered and may be wrong or contradictory; independent checking requires a responsible person to examine actual evidence, not just the record's wording.
3. A 'COMPLETE RECORD - HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED' V2 outcome is a self-reported record. An independent responsible person still needs to inspect the evidence, scope, consequences, permissions and required approvals. It is not a rollout authorisation.
4. The page keeps entries in memory, which is lost on reload or closing. Exported files may persist locally or in synced folders; device/browser recovery can also affect form state.
5. **Yes.** Simplifying the process, using existing tools, doing nothing for now and declining AI are acceptable outcomes.

## Observation categories

For each task and question, mark **clear without assistance**, **completed with assistance**, **incorrect or misunderstood**, or **not completed / not answered**. Quote a reader's own words where permission was given, rather than improving them into a more convincing response. Capture the help given, if any. A completion is not an 'approval' outcome.

For comprehension questions, also retain the original wording before any classification. If an answer is ambiguous, mark it ambiguous and explain why. The key is a guide to consistent review, not an absolute truth detector.

## Reporting boundaries

- Do not mark these materials as a completed independent review before real participation.
- Do not report a pass rate or representative sample without an appropriate design.
- Do not claim that this exercise establishes V2's safety, the AI method's efficacy or realised workplace benefits.
- Preserve unexpected failures, uncertainty and disagreements. Do not edit expected answers to make later results look favourable.
