# Independent reader review: AI Adoption Workbench

Status: **Optional materials, not conducted.** The materials here are proposed for an independent reader who has not helped design or test the workbench. There are no participants, observations, scores or findings yet.

Participation is entirely voluntary. **The owner is not recruiting or organising sessions.** These materials are available if someone independently offers to review the tool; no session is required to continue software testing or development. The main invitation is [public visitor feedback](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-adoption/discussions/new). Do not treat unsolicited comments as a controlled comprehension study.

## Purpose

Find out whether an ordinary reader can use the [live Workbench](https://shaunmarsden.github.io/practical-ai-adoption/workbench/) to record a work question, distinguish evidence from assumptions, recognise that V2 is separate and avoid treating a self-reported decision as permission.

This is a comprehension and navigation exercise, not workplace deployment testing, a validation of AI effectiveness or an examination of the reader.

## Setup

- Find one or more willing adults unfamiliar with the workbench. Seek their agreement before starting; participation is optional and can stop at any time.
- Give the reader **reviewer-pack.md only**. Do not give them the facilitator key or explain expected answers.
- Use the invented cases as written, not real employer or customer records. The reviewer may use any device and can follow links.
- Do not ask anyone to enter personal information, employer details or confidential data. Exported files stay on their own device unless they choose to share a redacted copy.
- Record whether a facilitator helped, and exactly what they said, before interpreting outcomes.
- Use the same tasks for each reader. Do not change a question halfway through a session; record proposed improvements separately.
- Capture a brief anonymous response per participant using the observation sheet. Ask permission before recording anything beyond written answers. Do not record a video, audio or screen without separate explicit agreement.
- Do not provide the scoring key until the independent answers are complete.

## Sequence

1. The reader opens the workbench and reads the initial information.
2. They complete three small fictional tasks from **reviewer-pack.md**. Ask them to proceed as they normally would, without coaching.
3. They export a text record if they are comfortable doing so. Record whether they found the function and could retrieve their file.
4. They answer five comprehension questions without hints.
5. After they finish, use **facilitator-key.md** to classify responses and record observed friction. Preserve all disagreements and unexpected answers.

Suggested session length is flexible, not a timed benchmark. If timing is measured, include the device, connection and whether help was given. Do not turn small, unrepresentative samples into a pass rate for general users.

## What to report

Report the actual number of readers, recruitment method, device/browser, whether they had seen the project before, each task's completion or failure, and the exact comprehension responses. Distinguish observation from facilitator inference. Report adverse findings and non-completion. With a small convenience sample, report individual results rather than statistical claims.

If no reader participates, the accurate conclusion remains **not conducted**.

## Boundaries

The workbench is a self-reported notebook, not an approval tool. The Pilot Readiness Assessment V2 is a separate self-reported assessment. Neither can verify fabricated evidence, permissions or external approval. Any separate reviewer of V2 should use its [existing independent review pack](../pilot-readiness-independent-review/README.md), not treat this exercise as validation of V2.
