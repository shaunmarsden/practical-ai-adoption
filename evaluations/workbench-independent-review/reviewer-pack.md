# Reader pack: using the AI Adoption Workbench

This is a voluntary usability exercise, not a test of you. We want to find unclear wording and difficult steps. There are no real companies, people, approvals or business outcomes in the examples below. Do not enter information from your own work.

Open the [AI Adoption Workbench](https://shaunmarsden.github.io/practical-ai-adoption/workbench/). You may consult its linked guides. Please work independently and tell the facilitator if you are stuck. If a step cannot be completed, leave it and explain what stopped you. You may stop at any time.

## Task A: A simpler option might be better

An invented community club sends a weekly activity summary. A volunteer copies the same public meeting date into three different templates. The dates are sometimes inconsistent because the templates are updated separately. There is no verified trial or time measurement.

In the workbench, record what is going wrong and compare an AI option with one plausible non-AI option. You do not need to run a pilot. Where a fact is not provided, mark it unknown rather than inventing it.

**Question A:** What option would you test or investigate first, and what missing fact could change that choice?

## Task B: A result with an unresolved problem

A fictional team tested AI-generated summaries using entirely invented queue items. It claims that accepted summaries were faster. It has not recorded the number of rejected outputs or the checking time. One invented summary contained a wrong deadline. Nobody has confirmed that the proposed tool is permitted for real operational data.

Use the workbench to record the claimed benefit, missing evidence, unresolved problem and appropriate next action. You may refer to the separate V2 assessment, but do not pretend you have completed it.

**Question B:** Does the faster-summary claim alone establish that wider use should begin? Why or why not?

## Task C: Record a decision without granting approval

A fictional reviewer has agreed that the team should **gather evidence using invented data only**, with no operational rollout. No organisation-wide approval has been sought or granted.

Record the human decision as accurately as the available choices allow. Leave scope and permission details unverified where they are unknown. Find the function that exports a text record and, if comfortable, download it.

**Question C:** Does the exported record itself approve a real-world deployment?

## Five comprehension questions

Answer in your own words. A short answer is fine.

1. Does completing all six sections mean the proposal is safe or approved?
2. What is the difference between a claim entered in the Workbench and independently checked evidence?
3. If the Workbench says that V2 has a complete record, what still needs to happen before wider use?
4. What happens to notes when the page is reloaded, and where might an exported file remain?
5. Can choosing a non-AI solution or deciding not to run a trial be a valid outcome?
