# AI pilot decisions: independent reviewer exercise

**Fictional examples only.** Please work from this document before opening the online tool or reading its methodology. This is about practical judgement, not spotting a single expected answer.

For each case, record:
- **Your decision:** withhold wider use; collect more evidence; send a complete case to an authorised human decision-maker; proceed only as a bounded offline learning exercise; or another outcome you explain.
- **Why:** the two or three facts driving your decision.
- **Next check/action:** what must happen before the situation changes.
- **Confidence/uncertainty:** any missing fact that could change your answer.

These choices are not promises that any route is legally or operationally authorised. If you believe a pilot itself needs to stop, say so.

## Comprehension check

Without reading the project documentation, explain in your own words:

1. What would you think **WITHHOLD EXPANSION** asks a manager to do? Does it also automatically require stopping an already authorised pilot?
2. What does **FURTHER ASSESSMENT REQUIRED** permit or not permit?
3. If a tool says **COMPLETE RECORD - HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED**, can someone deploy on that basis alone? Why?
4. Is **OFFLINE EXPERIMENT - NO ROLLOUT DECISION** the same as permission to process real customer data?
5. If a PDF contains ten Yes answers and referenced notes, what has actually been verified by the software?

Record your responses before opening the tool. You may say a label is unclear.

**Optional usability observation after answers are locked:** open the live tool using the built-in fictional example, explain what the output means, then describe one confusing step. The facilitator should observe rather than coach.
